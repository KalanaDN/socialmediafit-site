Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Your images stay on your device

SocialMediaFit processes images and video stills 100% locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos and graphics are never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone.

Accounts and personal data

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. The tool does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we do not collect personal data through the tool itself.

Advertising

The site is supported by third-party advertising (Adsterra). Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Hosting and server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider's servers may record standard access data (such as pages visited, browser type and IP address) in server logs for security and operation. We do not use server logs to identify individual users.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

No contact email is published on this site yet. If you need to reach the SocialMediaFit team about privacy, please check back — a contact address will be added here.
