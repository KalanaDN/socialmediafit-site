About SocialMediaFit

Written by the SocialMediaFit team — last updated 26 November 2026.

SocialMediaFit is a free online tool for creators. It checks your images and video stills against the real safe zones of TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Pinterest, Meta Threads and Bluesky — so your text, subtitles, logos and faces are never covered by the app's own buttons, captions and overlays.

Every platform covers a different part of the frame. SocialMediaFit draws the actual interface — top bar, right-hand engagement rail, bottom caption area — over your image, so you can see exactly what will be hidden before you post.

How it works

Upload an image (or a still from your video), pick the platform and aspect ratio, and the tool previews it with accurate UI overlays. Pan, zoom or add blurred background padding to fit, then export at the right size for that platform — or export a multi-ratio Creator Pack.

Privacy by design

SocialMediaFit runs 100% in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos and graphics never leave your device — there is no upload, no account and no sign-up. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Free, always

The tool is free to use. It is supported by advertising. Start with the safe zone checker, or read the free safe zone guides.
